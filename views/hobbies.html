Hobbies

Hockey

I've always been a hockey player growing up. I played organized travel hockey for 14 years of my life. I played for my high school team, capping my senior year off with a state championship ring, and an amazing run at the USA Hockey High School National Championships, making it all the way to the finals to win silver for 2A boys high school hockey. I'm a big Blackhawks fan, and I'm hopeful they can return to their former glory of the 2010s despite how bad it looks right now.
